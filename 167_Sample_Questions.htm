Final Exam Study Guide

Consolidated Sample Questions — All Subjects

167 Questions  |  Multiple Choice · True/False · Short Answer · Fill in the Blank

 

 

Instructor Sample Questions — From Class Audio

 

1.  (Multiple Choice)  In the Bible, Jesus Christ demonstrated his divine nature by:

A. Forgiving sins coupled with signs of healing

B. Playing poker with the devil after his time in the desert

C. Having knowledge of future events

D. All of the above

✓ Answer: A — Forgiving sins coupled with signs of healing.

2.  (Short Answer)  In a few words describe the offices of Christ as applied to or lived out by the lay faithful. Be sure to speak about the exercise of proper priority.

✓ Answer: Priest, Prophet, King — with proper priority: personal holiness first, then vocation/family, then temporal witness, then apostolate.

3.  (Short Answer)  List the four reasons for the Incarnation as given by the Catechism. (List exactly four — no more.)

✓ Answer: (1) To expiate sin / redeem us; (2) To show us God's infinite love; (3) To be a model of holiness; (4) To make us partakers of the divine nature (divinization). CCC 457–460.

4.  (True / False)  The persons of God all share an equal amount of divine essence.

✓ Answer: FALSE — each Person fully and wholly possesses the one divine nature; "share an equal amount" implies division, which is incorrect.

5.  (True / False)  Because God is not divided into parts, His mercy and justice are one and the same — they are convertible.

✓ Answer: TRUE.

6.  (Short Answer)  What is time?

✓ Answer: A measurement of change; the duration of that which changes. Time began with the creation of the universe.

7.  (True / False)  God can do everything.

✓ Answer: FALSE — God cannot lie, cannot act contrary to His own nature, and cannot sin. His omnipotence is not a license for contradiction.

8.  (True / False)  God has eternal and infallible foreknowledge of the free actions of men.

✓ Answer: TRUE — though strictly speaking God has knowledge, not "foreknowledge," since He exists outside of time.

9.  (Short Answer)  Provide a definition for "eternality" per Sheed.

✓ Answer: "The duration of that which simply IS" — applicable only to God (I AM). Not a long timeline but a timeless, perfect NOW (nunc stans).

10.  (Multiple Choice)  Christ is the ______ of all human history.

A. Key, center, and purpose

B. Proper interpreter

C. Plan and pattern of redemption

D. All of the above

✓ Answer: D — All of the above.

11.  (True / False)  Jesus Christ is true God and true man.

✓ Answer: TRUE.

12.  (True / False)  The hypostatic union is a mystery, strictly speaking. However, once it is revealed, it can be positively proven.

✓ Answer: FALSE — a strict theological mystery cannot be positively proven even after it is revealed.

13.  (True / False)  The hypostatic union, once it occurred, irrevocably changed the nature of God.

✓ Answer: FALSE — God is immutable; He does not change.

14.  (True / False)  In Jesus, there is a real duality of wills (human and divine) with a real moral unity.

✓ Answer: TRUE.

15.  (True / False)  Angels are personal beings.

✓ Answer: TRUE — they possess intellect and will, though not human nature.

16.  (True / False)  Angels were created in the beatific vision.

✓ Answer: FALSE — no creature is created in the beatific vision; it requires a free response of love.

17.  (True / False)  Human souls were all created in a single moment and stored in the Guff until a human body is made available to host it.

✓ Answer: FALSE — the "Guff" is a Jewish folk belief, not Catholic teaching. God creates each soul directly at the moment of conception.

18.  (Multiple Choice)  When Adam and Eve fell, it resulted in:

A. Harm to the body

B. Harm to the soul

C. Disruption of the created order

D. All of the above

✓ Answer: D — All of the above.

19.  (True / False)  A man can redeem himself by the practice of virtue.

✓ Answer: FALSE — grace is absolutely required for salvation. This is the heresy of Pelagianism.

20.  (Multiple Choice)  Jesus' office of king is best suited to healing man's ______.

A. Darkened intellect

B. Weakened will

C. Disordered love

✓ Answer: B — Weakened will. A king governs and rules; the kingly office inculcates obedience and strengthens the will.

21.  (True / False)  Jesus suffered on the cross to make atonement for all of humanity, including Himself.

✓ Answer: FALSE — He is sinless God; He had no need to atone for Himself.

22.  (Multiple Choice)  As King, Christ is the:

A. Lawgiver and judge

B. Judge but not lawgiver

C. Promise maker only (Luther's error)

D. None of the above describe Christ's kingship

✓ Answer: A — Lawgiver and judge. Council of Trent: anathema to those who say Christ is a Redeemer but not a lawgiver.

23.  (True / False)  The Church can be authentically described as the Mystical Body of Christ.

✓ Answer: TRUE — this is the most noble definition per Pope Pius XII, Mystici Corporis (1943).

24.  (Fill in the Blank)  The Church Suffering is comprised of ____________________.

✓ Answer: Those (the holy souls) in Purgatory.

25.  (True / False)  The Church was personally and directly instituted by the true historical Christ during His life among us.

✓ Answer: TRUE — not by Constantine in the 300s. The Oath Against Modernism (Pius X, 1910) affirmed this explicitly.

26.  (Multiple Choice)  The power of binding and loosing (Matthew 18:17) applies to which powers of the Church?

A. Legislative

B. Juridical

C. Punitive

D. All of the above

E. None of the above

✓ Answer: D — All of the above.

27.  (Multiple Choice)  The power to bind and loose also entails:

A. The power to consecrate the Eucharist

B. The power to forgive sins

C. The power to baptize

D. All of the above

E. None of the above

✓ Answer: D — All of the above.

28.  (True / False)  The Pope possesses full and supreme power of jurisdiction over the whole Church — not merely in matters of faith and morals, but also in Church discipline and in the government of the Church.

✓ Answer: TRUE — Pastor Aeternus (Vatican I, 1870).

29.  (True / False)  The Pope is not bound legally by ecclesiastical decisions, but only by divine law alone.

✓ Answer: TRUE — the Pope stands above canon law and can change it; he cannot change divine law.

30.  (True / False)  The lay faithful play no role in the saving mission of Christ whatsoever.

✓ Answer: FALSE — by Baptism all the faithful share in Christ's threefold office of Priest, Prophet, and King.

31.  (True / False)  As the final triumph of Christ, those in hell will be annihilated.

✓ Answer: FALSE — hell is permanent and eternal. Souls are immortal; they cannot be annihilated (this is the error of annihilationism).

32.  (Multiple Choice)  Which of the following is NOT a sign of the Second Coming?

A. Conversion of the Jews

B. A great apostasy

C. Appearance of the Antichrist

D. Nature will be in perfect harmony with man

✓ Answer: D — Nature in harmony with man describes the new heavens and new earth after the Second Coming, not a sign preceding it.

33.  (True / False)  Purgatory will not continue after the General Judgment.

✓ Answer: TRUE — this is the common teaching (sententia communis). At the Last Judgment purgatory ends.

34.  (Short Answer)  The Ark of the Covenant has three distinct contents that prefigure Mary as the New Ark and Jesus. What are those three contents, and how do they prefigure Christ?

✓ Answer: (1) Tablets of the Law → Christ the Lawgiver; (2) Rod of Aaron → Christ the King; (3) Manna → Christ the Bread of Life (John 6).

35.  (Short Answer)  In a single sentence, define Mary's perpetual virginity.

✓ Answer: Mary remained a virgin perpetually — before, during, and after the birth of Christ — throughout her entire life.

36.  (Multiple Choice)  Which of the following statements is TRUE about Mary as Mother of God?

A. Mary gave birth to the Person of the divine Word

B. The Council of Ephesus declared Mary as Christotokos only

C. Mary conceived and bore the Second Person of the Trinity according to the divine nature

D. None of the above are true

✓ Answer: A — Mary gave birth to the Person of the divine Word. You give birth to persons, not natures. (B is false — Theotokos, not Christotokos only. C is false.)

37.  (True / False)  Mary was not subject to inordinate desires (concupiscence), according to the common teaching of the Church.

✓ Answer: TRUE.

38.  (True / False)  The greeting "Hail, full of grace" functions as a proper name and represents a defining characteristic quality of Mary.

✓ Answer: TRUE — the Greek kecharitomene (perfect passive) is a scriptural defense of the Immaculate Conception.

39.  (Multiple Choice)  Concerning the Incarnation, the unconditioned predestination theory holds that:

A. The Incarnation would have happened even if the Fall did not occur

B. The primary purpose would then be the glory of God

C. It would be unfitting that sin — which God hates — should be the occasion for the greatest revelation of God

D. All of the above

✓ Answer: D — All of the above.

 

Divine Attributes — God's Nature and the Trinity

 

40.  (Multiple Choice)  Which Scripture verse is used to support the doctrine that God is Almighty?

A. Isaiah 40:28

B. Revelation 1:8

C. Exodus 3:14

D. John 14:6

✓ Answer: B — Revelation 1:8. God declares "I am the Alpha and the Omega… the Almighty."

41.  (Multiple Choice)  The phrase "Alpha and Omega" in Revelation 1:8 primarily signifies which aspect of God's nature?

A. God's role as judge

B. God's omniscience

C. God is eternal — the beginning and end of all things

D. God's love for humanity

✓ Answer: C — God is eternal, the beginning and end of all things, existing outside the limits of time.

42.  (Multiple Choice)  According to Sheed's Theology and Sanity, what is time fundamentally linked to?

A. The movement of planets

B. Human consciousness

C. The age of the universe

D. Change

✓ Answer: D — Sheed defines time as "the duration of that which changes."

43.  (Multiple Choice)  What Latin phrase does Sheed use to describe how God possesses His existence?

A. Ex nihilo

B. Tota simul

C. In persona Christi

D. Imago Dei

✓ Answer: B — Tota simul, meaning "all at once." God possesses His being not in successive moments but in one eternal Now.

44.  (Multiple Choice)  The doctrine that God is infinite means, according to Sheed, that God is limitless in which way(s)?

A. Only externally

B. Only internally

C. Both externally and internally — He has no parts and no limits

D. "Infinite" simply means very large

✓ Answer: C — Sheed explains infinite means limitless not only externally but internally: God has no parts.

45.  (Multiple Choice)  Which Scripture passage is cited to support the doctrine that God is infinite/boundless?

A. Isaiah 43:1

B. Malachi 3:6

C. Job 11:7–9

D. James 1:17

✓ Answer: C — Job 11:7–9: "Can you find out the limit of the Almighty?"

46.  (Multiple Choice)  In the doctrine "God is Being Itself," what is the key distinction between God and created beings?

A. God is more intelligent than created beings

B. God exists in time while creatures do not

C. God was created before all other things

D. In God, essence and existence are identical — He simply IS

✓ Answer: D — In God, essence and existence are identical. For creatures they are always distinct.

47.  (Multiple Choice)  Which Scripture passage most directly supports the doctrine that "God is Being Itself"?

A. Isaiah 45:5

B. Exodus 3:14

C. Malachi 3:6

D. Revelation 1:8

✓ Answer: B — Exodus 3:14. God reveals His name as "I AM WHO I AM."

48.  (Multiple Choice)  Why does Sheed argue that there can logically be only ONE God?

A. Because God revealed Himself only to one people

B. Because the universe was created in a single act

C. Because two infinite beings would each limit the other — making neither truly infinite

D. Because tradition has always held this to be true

✓ Answer: C — Infinity demands oneness. A second "god" would differ from the first by lacking something.

49.  (Multiple Choice)  Which verse is cited to support the doctrine that God is One?

A. Isaiah 40:28

B. John 14:6

C. Isaiah 45:5

D. Exodus 3:14

✓ Answer: C — Isaiah 45:5: "I am the LORD, and there is no other, besides me there is no God."

50.  (Multiple Choice)  When Jesus says "I am the way, the truth, and the life" (John 14:6), this reveals that:

A. God is a moral teacher who sets a good example

B. God merely tells the truth and never lies

C. God does not just conform to truth — He IS truth itself, the ultimate standard of reality

D. God's truth is only accessible through Scripture

✓ Answer: C — God's very being is the source and foundation of all reality.

51.  (Multiple Choice)  How does Sheed describe the "perfect Idea" God generates in knowing Himself?

A. A blueprint for creation

B. A set of moral laws given to humanity

C. An incomplete reflection of His nature

D. A perfect Word so complete it constitutes a divine Person — the Son

✓ Answer: D — God's perfect self-knowledge generates a complete "mental Word" that IS a divine Person: the Son.

52.  (Multiple Choice)  According to Isaiah 43:1, what actions demonstrate that God is personal?

A. He sends prophets and gives laws

B. He creates the universe and governs it

C. He forms, redeems, and calls each person by name

D. He punishes enemies and rewards the faithful

✓ Answer: C — God "formed you," "redeemed you," and "called you by name."

53.  (Multiple Choice)  Which Scripture verse directly supports the doctrine of God's immutability?

A. Malachi 3:6

B. Isaiah 45:5

C. John 14:6

D. Job 11:7–9

✓ Answer: A — Malachi 3:6: "For I the LORD do not change."

54.  (Multiple Choice)  Why is God's immutability significant for His covenant promises?

A. It means God will always punish sin without exception

B. Since God cannot gain or lose anything, His promises are eternally reliable and trustworthy

C. It means God's words carry legal weight in human courts

D. It means the covenant was established before creation

✓ Answer: B — Because God is perfectly complete and unchanging, His covenants are eternally reliable.

 

The Triune God

 

55.  (Multiple Choice)  What is the broadest definition of theology?

A. The study of the Bible and salvation history

B. The study of God in Himself only

C. The study of God in Himself AND all things related to Him

D. The study of how Christians should live morally

✓ Answer: C.

56.  (Multiple Choice)  The Cataphatic (positive) method of speaking about God means:

A. Saying what God is NOT

B. Making positive statements about God, always by analogy — with far more dissimilarity than similarity

C. Proving God's existence through reason alone

D. Describing God through mystical experience only

✓ Answer: B.

57.  (Multiple Choice)  What does "eternity" properly mean in this class?

A. An infinitely long timeline stretching in both directions

B. The state of never having been created

C. A perfect, timeless act of existence — a complete, indivisible "now"

D. A very long period of time after death

✓ Answer: C — Eternity is the nunc stans: a perfect, complete, timeless NOW — not time extended to infinity.

58.  (Multiple Choice)  The Latin phrase nunc stans refers to:

A. The eternal beginning of time

B. God's standing judgment over history

C. God's eternal present — the "standing now," as opposed to our flowing now (nunc fluens)

D. The moment of creation

✓ Answer: C.

59.  (Multiple Choice)  What is the key distinction between "nature" and "person"?

A. Nature is immortal; person is mortal

B. Nature says WHAT we are (source of operations); person says WHO we are (the one who acts)

C. Nature is spiritual; person is physical

D. Person belongs to God only; nature belongs to creatures

✓ Answer: B.

60.  (Multiple Choice)  The heresy of Arianism taught that:

A. The Father, Son, and Spirit are three separate and unequal Gods

B. The Son is a created being, not co-equal with the Father

C. The Persons of the Trinity are three "masks" God wears at different times

D. The Holy Spirit does not proceed from the Father

✓ Answer: B — "There was a time when the Son was not." Condemned at Nicaea, 325 AD.

61.  (Multiple Choice)  The heresy of Modalism (Sabellianism) teaches that:

A. There are three separate and unequal Gods

B. The Son is subordinate in nature to the Father

C. The Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are merely three "modes" or masks of one undifferentiated God — not genuinely distinct Persons

D. God cannot be personal

✓ Answer: C.

62.  (Multiple Choice)  Perichoresis refers to:

A. The eternal generation of the Son from the Father

B. The process of God creating the universe from nothing

C. The mutual interpenetration and coinherence of the three divine Persons — the "Divine Dance"

D. The attribution of works to individual Persons for pastoral purposes

✓ Answer: C.

63.  (Multiple Choice)  The term "consubstantial" (homoousios), defined at Nicaea, means:

A. The Son is similar in substance to the Father

B. The Son is of the SAME substance/nature as the Father — not merely like Him

C. The Son is greater in substance than the Holy Spirit

D. The Son is subordinate in nature but equal in dignity

✓ Answer: B — Semi-Arianism used homoiousios ("like substance") — one iota of difference that made it heresy.

64.  (Multiple Choice)  Why could the ancient Greek philosophers conclude through reason alone that there is only ONE God?

A. They had indirect access to the Hebrew Scriptures

B. A perfect being admits no second — any second "god" would differ from the first by lacking something, making neither truly perfect

C. The cosmos revealed three divine principles to them

D. Revelation was secretly communicated to Plato and Aristotle

✓ Answer: B.

65.  (Multiple Choice)  How many of the four key doctrinal statements about the Trinity must be held simultaneously?

A. Only the ones that don't seem to conflict

B. Two — oneness and distinction of Persons

C. Three — oneness, distinction, and unity of nature

D. All four — one God, absolutely distinct Persons, each fully possessing the one nature, yet only one God not three

✓ Answer: D — dropping any one leads to heresy.

66.  (Multiple Choice)  Which of the following best describes a "mystery" in the proper theological sense?

A. A contradiction that must simply be accepted without thought

B. Something completely unknowable even after revelation

C. A reality that cannot be fully comprehended by finite intellects — distinct from a contradiction, and knowable in part through revelation

D. A deliberate secret God chose never to disclose

✓ Answer: C.

67.  (Multiple Choice)  What does "appropriation" mean in Trinitarian theology?

A. Proving one Person is greater than the others

B. Dividing God's nature equally among the three Persons

C. Attributing a divine work primarily to one Person for pedagogical purposes, even though all three act in every external work

D. The process by which human souls are united to God in heaven

✓ Answer: C.

68.  (Multiple Choice)  In the Trinity, there are:

A. Three natures and one Person

B. One nature and one Person expressed in three ways

C. Three Persons and three natures, fully united

D. Three Persons and one nature — each Person fully possessing the undivided divine nature

✓ Answer: D.

69.  (True / False)  Natural theology alone is sufficient to discover that God is a Trinity of Persons.

✓ Answer: FALSE — the Trinity is entirely hidden from unaided reason and required divine self-disclosure.

70.  (True / False)  Because God is a pure spirit with no parts, He is non-spatial and therefore "nowhere."

✓ Answer: FALSE — God is omnipresent, present everywhere by operation (sustaining all things in existence), not spatially.

71.  (True / False)  Jesus Christ is a human person who also possesses a divine nature.

✓ Answer: FALSE — Christ is a DIVINE Person (the Second Person of the Trinity) who possesses two natures. He is fully human but is NOT a human person.

72.  (True / False)  In the Trinity, the three Persons share the one divine nature in the sense that it is divided equally among them.

✓ Answer: FALSE — the divine nature is not divided. Each Person fully and wholly possesses the one undivided divine nature.

73.  (True / False)  God's foreknowledge that a soul will be damned means He predestined that soul for hell.

✓ Answer: FALSE — knowing is not causing. God exists outside time and knows all outcomes, but damnation results from the soul's own free rejection of God.

74.  (True / False)  God's justice and mercy are ultimately identical in God, even though we make useful pastoral distinctions between them.

✓ Answer: TRUE — the divine attributes (justice, mercy, love, wisdom) are "the convertibles" — identical in God. Our distinctions are useful but approximate.

 

Christology — The Hypostatic Union and Heresies

 

75.  (Multiple Choice)  What is the broadest definition of theology?

A. Soteriology

B. The study of the Incarnation only

C. The study of God in Himself and all things related to Him

D. The study of the Bible

✓ Answer: C — included here as review: soteriology is the study of God's saving action through Christ.

76.  (Multiple Choice)  The four reasons God became flesh (CCC 457–460) include all of the following EXCEPT:

A. To expiate our sins and redeem us

B. To demonstrate that matter is evil and the spirit alone matters

C. To show us the love of the Father

D. To make us partakers of the divine nature (divinization)

✓ Answer: B — the Incarnation consecrates matter as good, doing the opposite of B.

77.  (Multiple Choice)  The Council of Chalcedon (451 AD) defined that Christ's two natures come together:

A. With confusion and change, forming a third hybrid nature

B. Without confusion, change, division, or separation

C. With the human nature absorbed into the divine

D. With the divine nature temporarily suspended during the Passion

✓ Answer: B — the definitive Chalcedonian formula.

78.  (Multiple Choice)  The term "hypostatic union" refers to:

A. The union of the Father and Son in the Trinity

B. The union of Christ's soul and body at the Resurrection

C. The substantial union of two complete natures — divine and human — in the one divine Person of the Son

D. The union of Christ's human will with the divine will

✓ Answer: C.

79.  (Multiple Choice)  The heresy of Adoptionism teaches that:

A. Jesus pre-existed as a semi-divine being before taking on a human body

B. Jesus is a virtuous human being upon whom God poured His grace — adopted as "Son of God" but not inherently and eternally divine

C. Jesus appeared to be human but was only a divine projection

D. Jesus had a divine nature but no human soul

✓ Answer: B.

80.  (Multiple Choice)  The heresy of Docetism's core claim is:

A. Christ had two persons operating within one body

B. Christ's divine nature was absorbed into His human nature

C. Christ only appeared to be human — His body was an illusion or projection, not real flesh

D. Christ was the greatest of all creatures but not truly God

✓ Answer: C — dokein means "to seem." Rooted in Gnostic contempt for matter.

81.  (Multiple Choice)  St. Gregory of Nazianzus' phrase "That which was not assumed was not saved" refutes:

A. Nestorianism

B. Arianism

C. Apollinarianism and Docetism — both deny that Christ took on a complete human nature

D. Monophysitism

✓ Answer: C — if He had no real body or no human soul, our bodies and souls are not redeemed.

82.  (Multiple Choice)  The communicatio idiomatum (Communication of Idioms) directly refutes:

A. Arianism

B. Nestorianism — because it shows that what is true of Christ in His human nature can be truly said of God

C. Docetism

D. Monophysitism

✓ Answer: B — because there is ONE Person with two real natures, "God was born" and "Mary is Mother of God" are legitimate.

83.  (Multiple Choice)  Apollinarianism (condemned at Constantinople, 381 AD) held that:

A. Christ had two persons

B. The divine Word assumed only a human body — the divine intellect replacing the human rational soul

C. Christ's human nature dissolved into the divine

D. Christ was adopted as Son of God at baptism

✓ Answer: B.

84.  (Multiple Choice)  Monophysitism (condemned at Chalcedon, 451 AD) holds that:

A. Christ had only one person but genuinely two natures

B. The divine nature absorbs and eliminates the human nature — leaving only one nature

C. Christ had two wills but only one nature

D. Mary is not the Mother of God

✓ Answer: B — mono (one) + physis (nature).

85.  (Multiple Choice)  The Church's orthodox response to Monothelitism (condemned 681 AD) is:

A. Christ has one will that operates in two modes

B. Because Christ has two complete natures each including a will, He has both a divine will and a genuine human will — in perfect moral harmony

C. Christ's human will is identical to His divine will

D. The question of Christ's wills is an open theological question

✓ Answer: B — Matthew 26:39 ("not my will, but yours be done") is the key scriptural proof.

86.  (Multiple Choice)  The name "Jesus" (from the Hebrew Yeshua) means:

A. "Son of God"

B. "Anointed One"

C. "God saves"

D. "Lord of Lords"

✓ Answer: C.

87.  (Multiple Choice)  Christ is called the "Anointed One" because He fulfills the three anointed offices of the Old Testament. What are they?

A. King, warrior, and judge

B. Priest, prophet, and king

C. Lawgiver, priest, and healer

D. Prophet, scribe, and shepherd

✓ Answer: B.

88.  (Multiple Choice)  The title "Lord" (Kyrios) applied to Jesus is significant because:

A. It was a standard polite title for any respected teacher

B. In the Greek Old Testament, Kyrios replaced the divine name YHWH — applying it to Jesus directly identifies Him with the God of Israel

C. It refers specifically to His political kingship

D. It was first used at the Transfiguration

✓ Answer: B.

89.  (True / False)  Jesus Christ is a human person who also possesses a divine nature.

✓ Answer: FALSE — He is a DIVINE Person who possesses two natures. He is fully human but NOT a human person. (This is repeated as a critical exam point.)

90.  (True / False)  The hypostatic union is permanent — right now, seated at the right hand of the Father, there is a real human body and soul united to the divine Person of the Son, forever.

✓ Answer: TRUE.

91.  (True / False)  Docetism — the denial of Christ's real human body — has no practical relevance today.

✓ Answer: FALSE — crypto-docetist tendencies persist: dismissing the body as bad, rejecting sacramentality, treating matter as spiritually irrelevant.

92.  (True / False)  The Catholic Church holds that Jesus had genuine human knowledge that grew through experience (Luke 2:52) AND that His human intellect possessed the beatific vision from the moment of conception.

✓ Answer: TRUE — both are held simultaneously. Two different modes of knowing in the one Person.

 

Creation, Angels, and Original Sin

 

93.  (Multiple Choice)  Humani Generis (1950) was issued by which pope, and for what primary purpose?

A. Pope John XXIII — to open the Church to modern scientific consensus

B. Pope Pius XII — to warn against false opinions threatening Catholic doctrine

C. Pope Leo XIII — to address faith and reason

D. Pope Paul VI — to clarify teaching after Vatican II

✓ Answer: B — Pope Pius XII, August 12, 1950.

94.  (Multiple Choice)  Regarding the origin of the human body, Humani Generis teaches that:

A. Evolution is a proven fact Catholics must accept

B. Any discussion of evolution is forbidden

C. Research on the evolution of the human body from pre-existing matter is permitted, but each human soul must be held as immediately and directly created by God

D. Both body and soul evolved through purely natural processes

✓ Answer: C.

95.  (Multiple Choice)  Humani Generis definitively rejects polygenism because:

A. Science has proven there was only one original human couple

B. It cannot be reconciled with the doctrine of Original Sin, which requires a sin truly committed by one Adam, transmitted to all by generation

C. Genesis uses only singular language

D. All of the above

✓ Answer: B — the doctrinal reason is the key.

96.  (Multiple Choice)  God creates ex nihilo. This means:

A. God creates from pre-existing chaos

B. God creates by organizing pre-existing energy

C. God creates from absolutely nothing — no pre-existing matter, space, or assistance required

D. God creates through natural processes over long periods

✓ Answer: C — ex nihilo is a defined dogma.

97.  (Multiple Choice)  Why does God create, according to Catholic teaching?

A. Because He needed companions

B. Because creation was a necessary overflow of His nature

C. Freely, out of love, to manifest and share His glory — not out of need or necessity

D. To populate a universe that already existed

✓ Answer: C — God creates freely and gratuitously (CCC 293).

98.  (Multiple Choice)  The lasting effects of the Fall passed on to all human beings include:

A. Loss of sanctifying grace, darkened intellect, weakened will, disordered passions (concupiscence), suffering, and death

B. Loss of free will and the ability to choose good

C. Complete corruption of human nature so that nothing good remains

D. Only physical death — spiritual effects were immediately restored

✓ Answer: A — human nature is wounded but not totally corrupted.

99.  (Multiple Choice)  The Protoevangelium (Genesis 3:15) is understood by the Church as:

A. A curse with no redemptive meaning

B. The first proclamation of the Good News — God's promise of enmity between the serpent and the woman, and her offspring's ultimate victory

C. A symbolic description of natural predation

D. A warning to future generations about snakes

✓ Answer: B — CCC 410–411. Points forward to Christ (New Adam) and Mary (New Eve).

100.  (True / False)  Humani Generis teaches that Catholics are completely free to accept or reject the doctrine of a real, historical Adam as the single first parent of all humanity.

✓ Answer: FALSE — Humani Generis explicitly states Catholics do not enjoy freedom on polygenism. A real individual Adam is required by the defined doctrine of Original Sin.

101.  (True / False)  God continuously sustains creation in existence at every moment — without His continuous act of sustaining it, creation would simply cease to be.

✓ Answer: TRUE — this is the doctrine of conservation (CCC 301).

102.  (True / False)  Original Sin is transmitted to all humans by imitation — because we imitate what Adam did.

✓ Answer: FALSE — this was the heresy of Pelagius. Original Sin is transmitted by generation (by being born into the human family), not by imitation (CCC 404).

103.  (True / False)  The fallen angels retain their angelic intelligence and power after their fall — they simply use it in opposition to God and against human souls.

✓ Answer: TRUE — the fall did not strip demons of their angelic nature (CCC 391–395).

 

Soteriology — God's Saving Action Through Jesus Christ

 

104.  (Multiple Choice)  The "Unconditioned Predestination Theory" holds that:

A. God was compelled to become incarnate because of the gravity of sin

B. The Incarnation would have occurred even if Adam and Eve had never sinned — primarily for the glory of God

C. Christ's Incarnation was predestined only for those who cooperate with grace

D. Salvation is fully accomplished without any human cooperation

✓ Answer: B — Duns Scotus and the Scotist school.

105.  (Multiple Choice)  The "Conditioned Predestination Theory" is supported by all of the following EXCEPT:

A. 1 Timothy 1:15 — "Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners"

B. Matthew 1:21 — "He will save his people from their sins"

C. The Easter Vigil Exsultet — "O happy fault, which merited for us so great a Redeemer"

D. John 17:4 — "I have glorified you on earth; I have finished the work you gave me to do"

✓ Answer: D — John 17:4 is the Scotist proof text for the unconditioned theory.

106.  (Multiple Choice)  "Objective Redemption" refers to:

A. Each individual's personal decision to accept Christ

B. The Church's official teaching on salvation

C. The totality of everything Christ did from conception through Ascension — winning all grace necessary for our salvation

D. The minimum requirements for receiving the sacraments worthily

✓ Answer: C.

107.  (Multiple Choice)  "Subjective Redemption" means:

A. Christ's deeply personal emotional suffering

B. Private theological opinions about salvation

C. The merits won by Christ must be personally applied and appropriated by each individual through cooperation with grace

D. Salvation is guaranteed once a person is baptized

✓ Answer: C.

108.  (Multiple Choice)  Why can a finite human being NOT make atonement for sin on his own?

A. Because humans are too distracted by earthly pleasures

B. Because while sin is a finite deed, it is an offense against an infinite God — making the debt infinite — and a finite creature cannot generate infinite atonement

C. Because God arbitrarily decided to require a divine sacrifice

D. Because Adam's sin made humans incapable of any meritorious act

✓ Answer: B — the philosophical proof for the Incarnation.

109.  (Multiple Choice)  The heretic Pelagius taught that:

A. Only God could save us — human effort is irrelevant

B. The Fall completely destroyed human nature

C. Only the body was harmed by the Fall; the soul remained intact enough to achieve salvation through natural virtue alone, without grace

D. Christ's sacrifice was sufficient only for baptized Christians

✓ Answer: C — condemned. Virtue is valuable but grace is absolutely required.

110.  (Multiple Choice)  The four reasons for the Incarnation (CCC 457–460) are:

A. To demonstrate power, establish the Church, fulfill prophecy, and defeat the devil

B. To expiate sin, reveal God's love, give us a model of holiness, and make us partakers of the divine nature

C. To establish the priesthood, write the New Testament, found a new religion, and judge the living and dead

D. All of the above

✓ Answer: B — these are exam-required material.

111.  (Multiple Choice)  Christ's Prophetic Office heals which wound of the Fall?

A. The weakened will

B. Disordered passions and concupiscence

C. The darkened intellect

D. The loss of physical immortality

✓ Answer: C — Christ as the mouthpiece of God heals religious ignorance and the darkened intellect.

112.  (Multiple Choice)  Christ's Kingly Office heals which wound of the Fall?

A. The darkened intellect

B. The weakened will — by giving the divine law and inculcating a spirit of obedience

C. Physical death and suffering

D. The loss of sanctifying grace

✓ Answer: B.

113.  (Multiple Choice)  Pope Innocent X (1653) condemned which error about the scope of Christ's redemption?

A. That Christ's death redeemed all of creation including animals

B. That Christ died for all humanity

C. That Christ died only for the predestined or those who would cooperate with His grace

D. That purgatory exists

✓ Answer: C — Calvinist doctrine of limited atonement, condemned. 1 John 2:2: "He is the offering for the whole world."

114.  (True / False)  The "Paschal Mystery" always refers strictly to Christ's suffering, death, and Resurrection — nothing more.

✓ Answer: FALSE — most often it refers to those three, but in CCC and theology it can encompass the totality of the Incarnation from conception through Ascension.

115.  (True / False)  God was obligated to save humanity — given His infinite love, He had no choice but to redeem us.

✓ Answer: FALSE — God is absolutely sovereign. He freely and gratuitously chose to save us. Presuming He must is a sin against His sovereignty.

116.  (True / False)  Christ's cross removes the eternal punishment for sin entirely, while temporal punishment remains and must be addressed through penance, indulgences, and patient suffering.

✓ Answer: TRUE — this distinction is the foundation of the theology of penance, indulgences, and purgatory.

117.  (True / False)  At the final consummation, Christ's sacrifice will be perpetually re-offered in heaven as the eternal Mass, since His priesthood is eternal.

✓ Answer: FALSE — the sacrifice is complete and finished. What is eternal is the consummation of the sacrifice (the Beatific Vision), not the ongoing sacrificial act. The Book of Revelation shows an altar of incense, not a sacrificial altar.

 

Ecclesiology — The Church

 

118.  (Multiple Choice)  According to Mystici Corporis (1943), the most noble definition of the Church is:

A. A divinely established institution governed by the Pope and bishops

B. A voluntary assembly of believers who follow Jesus

C. The Mystical Body of Christ — with Christ as Head and the baptized as members joined through Baptism

D. The visible sign of God's kingdom, administered through the sacraments

✓ Answer: C.

119.  (Multiple Choice)  The "Church Triumphant," "Church Suffering," and "Church Militant" refer respectively to:

A. The hierarchy, laity, and clergy

B. The saints in heaven (and angels), the holy souls in Purgatory, and the baptized faithful on earth

C. The Eastern Church, Western Church, and Church in mission territories

D. The canonized, those in RCIA, and regular Mass-goers

✓ Answer: B.

120.  (Multiple Choice)  Full membership in the Church (Mystici Corporis) requires:

A. Confirmation, Eucharist, and obedience to the Pope

B. Valid Baptism, profession of the true Faith as the Church teaches it, and communion with the Pope

C. Moral living, weekly Mass, and financial support of the parish

D. Belief in Jesus, Baptism, and personal conviction of salvation

✓ Answer: B — all three conditions from Mystici Corporis, para. 22.

121.  (Multiple Choice)  The three "visible signs of unity" required for a community to be called "Church" (not merely an "ecclesial community") are:

A. A Pope, college of cardinals, and a cathedral

B. Profession of the full Catholic faith, celebration of all seven sacraments, and true apostolic succession

C. Valid Baptism, Sunday Mass attendance, and annual Confession

D. A creed, a Bible, and a recognized ministry

✓ Answer: B.

122.  (Multiple Choice)  The three powers Christ transferred to the Apostles (and thus to the Church's hierarchy) are:

A. Power to perform miracles, read Scripture, and baptize

B. Preaching/prophetic power; legislative, juridical, and punitive power; and priestly/sanctifying power

C. Power to canonize saints, define dogma, and convene councils

D. All of the above

✓ Answer: B.

123.  (Multiple Choice)  Which group possesses apostolic succession and valid sacraments but remains separated from the Catholic Church?

A. Lutherans

B. Anglicans

C. Greek Orthodox

D. Baptists

✓ Answer: C — the Orthodox are technically a Church but separated due to rejection of the Pope's primacy.

124.  (Multiple Choice)  The four conditions for a papal statement to be ex cathedra (infallible) include all of the following EXCEPT:

A. He is acting as shepherd and teacher of all Christians

B. He defines a doctrine by virtue of his supreme apostolic authority

C. The doctrine concerns faith or morals to be held by the universal Church

D. The statement is approved by a two-thirds vote of the College of Bishops

✓ Answer: D — infallibility belongs to the Petrine office alone; no bishop vote is required.

125.  (Multiple Choice)  How is the Pope's power of jurisdiction over the whole Church best described?

A. Conditional — dependent on approval of an ecumenical council

B. Delegated — given temporarily by the College of Bishops

C. Ordinary and immediate — the normal, permanent state of governance requiring no intermediary

D. Supreme only in matters of defined dogma

✓ Answer: C — Pastor Aeternus (1870).

126.  (Multiple Choice)  According to Lumen Gentium §16, the Church's relationship to the Jewish people is:

A. The Jewish covenant has been revoked since Christ's coming

B. They remain most dear to God — He does not repent of His gifts or His call; they are still the chosen people awaiting the fullness of the faith

C. They are spiritually equivalent to Muslims

D. Their salvation depends entirely on formal conversion to Catholicism

✓ Answer: B — Romans 11: God does not repent of His gifts or His calls.

127.  (True / False)  Excommunication removes the baptismal character and makes a person no longer a member of the Church in any sense.

✓ Answer: FALSE — excommunication separates from the benefits and communion of the Church, but the indelible baptismal character remains.

128.  (True / False)  The Pope is bound by both divine law and ecclesiastical (canon) law.

✓ Answer: FALSE — partially. The Pope is bound by divine law (eternal and natural law) but NOT by ecclesiastical law, which he can change or dispense from.

129.  (True / False)  A bishop's authority over his diocese is merely delegated to him by the Pope.

✓ Answer: FALSE — a bishop's authority is proper, ordinary, and immediate — given by divine right through episcopal ordination. It is regulated by the Pope but not derived from him.

130.  (True / False)  The Catholic Church teaches that other religions are legitimate parallel paths to salvation alongside Christianity.

✓ Answer: FALSE — there is ONE Savior, Jesus Christ. Other religions contain elements of truth but cannot save. God can save outside visible boundaries, but the ordinary and sure means is the Church and her sacraments.

131.  (True / False)  The lay faithful participate in Christ's threefold office of Priest, Prophet, and King by virtue of their Baptism.

✓ Answer: TRUE — Baptism confers a real (though distinct) participation in all three offices (1 Peter 2:9).

 

Eschatology — The Last Things

 

132.  (Multiple Choice)  How does the Catechism define death?

A. The cessation of all biological functions

B. The separation of the soul from the body

C. The moment of permanent loss of consciousness

D. The transition to another state of existence

✓ Answer: B — CCC 624, 650, 997.

133.  (Multiple Choice)  Death entered human existence because:

A. Death is the natural endpoint of biological organisms, planned from the beginning

B. Death was introduced as a mercy to limit suffering

C. Adam and Eve's original sin brought death as punishment — before the Fall humans were immortal

D. Death results from the natural weakness of matter

✓ Answer: C — de fide (defined dogma).

134.  (Multiple Choice)  According to Benedictus Deus (1336), what happens to a soul who dies in actual mortal sin?

A. Enters temporary purification before final judgment

B. Enters hell immediately after death

C. Sleeps until the General Judgment

D. Receives one final opportunity to choose God

✓ Answer: B — immediately, with no waiting period or second chance.

135.  (Multiple Choice)  The essential happiness of heaven is:

A. Reunion with loved ones, communion with saints, and the glorified body

B. Freedom from all suffering and eternal rest

C. The immediate, unmediated, face-to-face vision of God — the Beatific Vision

D. All of the above equally

✓ Answer: C — the Beatific Vision is the essential happiness. Everything else is accidental happiness.

136.  (Multiple Choice)  There are degrees of perfection in heaven. Which best describes this teaching?

A. Only martyrs receive higher glory

B. Degrees are proportioned to each person's merits; each soul is filled to its own capacity and fully satisfied — no envy or rivalry

C. All souls experience identical beatitude

D. Degrees apply only to angels, not humans

✓ Answer: B — de fide (Council of Trent, Session 6; Decree for the Greeks, 1439).

137.  (Multiple Choice)  The "pain of loss" — the principal punishment of hell — means:

A. The physical torment of fire

B. Eternal separation from God — exclusion from the Beatific Vision, missing the entire point of existence

C. The anguish of knowing others are in heaven

D. The loss of one's physical body until the General Resurrection

✓ Answer: B — CCC 1035.

138.  (Multiple Choice)  The heresy of apokatastasis (condemned) teaches that:

A. Hell involves only spiritual separation, not physical suffering

B. Souls in hell are eventually annihilated

C. All rational creatures — including the damned and the devil — are ultimately saved and restored to God

D. The Second Coming inaugurates a literal 1,000-year earthly reign of Christ

✓ Answer: C — taught by Origen; condemned. Hell is permanent (de fide).

139.  (Multiple Choice)  The two conditions that cause a soul to enter Purgatory are:

A. Dying without Last Rites, or dying outside the Church

B. Venial sin still on the soul at death, OR temporal punishment still owed even after confession

C. Dying with unconfessed mortal sins later forgiven by perfect contrition

D. Dying before completing assigned penances

✓ Answer: B.

140.  (Multiple Choice)  The strongest direct Scriptural support for Purgatory is:

A. John 3:16

B. Matthew 16:18

C. 2 Maccabees 12:42–49 — Judas Maccabeus collects money for sin offerings for fallen soldiers

D. Romans 8:28

✓ Answer: C — prayer for the dead presupposes a state where prayer can help. 1 Cor 3:13–15 is the key New Testament companion.

141.  (Multiple Choice)  Which of the following is NOT a sign of the Second Coming?

A. Preaching of the Gospel to the whole world

B. A great apostasy — many faithful falling away

C. The appearance of a series of three Antichrists in succession

D. Conversion of the Jews and great persecution of the just

✓ Answer: C — there is one Antichrist; the notion of three in succession is not Catholic teaching.

142.  (Multiple Choice)  The General (Final) Judgment will involve:

A. God assigning new fates differing from particular judgment

B. Every action of every rational being disclosed — and how God wove grace through those actions revealed

C. Re-examination of cases for those still in Purgatory

D. Annihilation of hell and transfer of all souls to heaven

✓ Answer: B — a public disclosure of all history, not a reversal of particular judgment.

143.  (Multiple Choice)  The four properties of the glorified resurrected body are:

A. Invisibility, weightlessness, omnipresence, omniscience

B. Impassibility, subtlety, agility, and clarity

C. Immortality, beauty, wisdom, and perfect memory

D. Incorruptibility, transparency, flight, and divine knowledge

✓ Answer: B — all four patterned on the risen Christ.

144.  (Multiple Choice)  Concerning the end of the world, Catholic teaching holds:

A. The world will be annihilated and replaced with purely spiritual existence

B. The present world will be both destroyed AND renewed — transformed to serve glorified bodies in a state better than its original condition

C. The physical universe continues unchanged as home of the blessed

D. The end of the world is a metaphor for personal death

✓ Answer: B — both sententia certa: destroyed (2 Pet 3:10) and restored (CCC 1047; Rev 21:1).

145.  (True / False)  The Church teaches there is a "second chance" after death — a soul who dies without repentance can still choose God during a transitional review process.

✓ Answer: FALSE — the time for merit ends at death (sententia certa). Particular judgment is immediate and final.

146.  (True / False)  In heaven, the theological virtues of faith and hope continue to operate just as they do on earth.

✓ Answer: FALSE — faith and hope cease because their object (something not yet possessed) is now directly and fully possessed. Charity remains and is perfected.

147.  (True / False)  The "good thief" (Luke 23:43) is a strong argument against the existence of Purgatory.

✓ Answer: FALSE — after Jesus died, the good thief's legs were broken and he died in agony, effectively experiencing a purgatory on earth. The promise was fulfilled; his purification was completed through his agonizing death.

148.  (True / False)  The bodily resurrection means each person will receive numerically the same body they had on earth — their own specific body, not a substitute.

✓ Answer: TRUE — de fide. "Numerically the same" means enough of one's own physical matter to be recognizably and truly that person's body.

149.  (True / False)  Millenarianism — the belief that Christ will physically reign on earth for a literal 1,000 years with temple worship restored — is a legitimate Catholic theological opinion.

✓ Answer: FALSE — chiliastic millenarianism is explicitly condemned (CCC 676). Two legitimate positions exist: Augustine's (Church era) and a sabbath-rest reading (Irenaeus/Justin Martyr). Neither involves Christ physically reigning in the flesh for a literal thousand years.

 

Mariology — Our Lady: Prefigurements, Dogmas, and Proper Veneration

 

150.  (Multiple Choice)  The Ark of the Covenant is a type of Mary. The three contents correspond in all of the following ways EXCEPT:

A. The Ten Commandments → Mary carries the Lawgiver Himself in her womb

B. The Manna → Mary carries Jesus Christ, the true Bread of Life (John 6)

C. The Rod of Aaron → Mary carries the source of all divine authority and life

D. The Mercy Seat → Mary carries the blood of the new covenant in her own veins

✓ Answer: D — the Mercy Seat connection is not made in the outline.

151.  (Multiple Choice)  The parallel between the Ark of the Covenant (2 Samuel 6) and the Visitation (Luke 1) includes all of the following EXCEPT:

A. Both events occur in the hill country of Judea

B. Both the Ark and Mary remain at their respective locations for three months

C. Both the Ark and Mary are greeted with trumpet blasts

D. David dances before the Ark; John the Baptist leaps in Elizabeth's womb

✓ Answer: C — trumpet blasts are not among the parallels identified.

152.  (Multiple Choice)  The Eve/Mary parallel (New Eve typology) holds that:

A. Both Eve and Mary were conceived without original sin, making them equal in holiness

B. Eve, created in original holiness, was visited by a fallen angel and said yes to evil; Mary, conceived without sin, was visited by Gabriel and said yes to God — reversing the Fall

C. Both women made free choices but only Mary's mattered

D. Eve and Mary are spiritually the same person — Mary is Eve reincarnated

✓ Answer: B — Mary's obedient fiat initiates redemption; she is titled "Cause of Our Joy."

153.  (Multiple Choice)  The Immaculate Conception (Pius IX, Ineffabilis Deus, 1854) states Mary was preserved from original sin:

A. At the moment of the Annunciation

B. From the moment of her birth

C. From the moment of her conception — by singular grace, in view of the merits of Jesus Christ

D. Through her own lifelong cooperation with grace

✓ Answer: C — "at the first instant of her conception." Not at birth, not at the Annunciation.

154.  (Multiple Choice)  "Prevenient grace" in relation to the Immaculate Conception means:

A. Mary received more graces throughout her life than anyone else

B. Rather than pulling Mary out of the pit of sin after she fell, Christ bridged her life over the pit entirely — His saving grace applied to her preventively

C. Mary's grace came before Christ, making her salvation independent of Him

D. Mary was given advance notice of her special role

✓ Answer: B — she was saved by Christ, but preventively rather than remedially.

155.  (Multiple Choice)  The Greek word kecharitomene (Luke 1:28, "full of grace") is significant because:

A. It was a common greeting for holy women

B. It is a perfect passive verb — indicating an action completed in the past whose effects continue permanently — providing scriptural grounding for the Immaculate Conception

C. It was a title reserved for the High Priest

D. It is the same word used for the outpouring of grace at Pentecost

✓ Answer: B — the Greek perfect passive tense is the scriptural basis for the Church's teaching.

156.  (Multiple Choice)  The dogma of the Divine Maternity (Theotokos) was defined at Ephesus (431 AD) against which heresy?

A. Arianism

B. Nestorianism — which held Mary could only be called Christotokos because God cannot be born, effectively dividing the two natures into two persons

C. Docetism

D. Apollinarianism

✓ Answer: B — key principle: you give birth to persons, not natures.

157.  (Multiple Choice)  The Queen Mother (Gebirah) typology applies to Mary because:

A. Mary was the most powerful woman in first-century Israel

B. In the Davidic kingdom, the queen was the king's mother (not wife) — a formal office of honor and intercession; since Jesus is the Davidic King, Mary is Queen Mother

C. Mary was appointed queen by the Apostles after the Ascension

D. The title "queen" is purely metaphorical

✓ Answer: B — 1 Kings 2:19: Solomon rises to meet Bathsheba, bows, and seats her at his right hand.

158.  (Multiple Choice)  Mary's Perpetual Virginity is:

A. A pious tradition but not a defined dogma

B. A defined dogma (de fide), affirmed by the Lateran Synod of 649 and multiple councils

C. A teaching applying only to spiritual virginity, not bodily intactness

D. A post-Vatican II development

✓ Answer: B — Luther himself held this teaching.

159.  (Multiple Choice)  When the Gospels refer to Jesus' "brothers" (Matthew 12:46), the Catholic response is:

A. These were Mary and Joseph's children — the Church has no teaching against this

B. The Greek adelphos can mean cousins or kinsmen; also, Jesus would not have entrusted Mary to John (John 19:27) if blood brothers existed to care for her

C. These were Joseph's children from a previous marriage — the defined teaching

D. Scripture uses "brothers" loosely to mean any followers of Jesus

✓ Answer: B — two convergent arguments: linguistic and narrative.

160.  (Multiple Choice)  The Bodily Assumption (Pius XII, Munificentissimus Deus, 1950) is significant because:

A. It proves Mary never died since death is a punishment for sin and she was sinless

B. Mary prefigures the destiny of the whole Church — she already fully possesses (body and soul glorified) what all believers await at the Second Coming

C. It established Mary as co-redemptrix alongside Christ

D. It was the first time the Church taught Mary was in heaven

✓ Answer: B.

161.  (Multiple Choice)  The three levels of veneration are latria, dulia, and hyperdulia. Which correctly describes all three?

A. Latria = prayer; dulia = song; hyperdulia = silent contemplation

B. Latria = worship owed to God alone; dulia = reverence owed to angels and saints; hyperdulia = super-reverence owed to Mary alone as Queen above all

C. Latria = relics; dulia = icons; hyperdulia = the Eucharist

D. All three are forms of worship, distinguished only by intensity

✓ Answer: B — the difference between latria and hyperdulia is like an apple vs. an orange, not a large vs. small apple.

162.  (True / False)  The Immaculate Conception refers to the virginal conception of Jesus in Mary's womb.

✓ Answer: FALSE — this is the most common misunderstanding. The Immaculate Conception refers to Mary's own conception — her preservation from original sin. The Virgin Birth refers to Jesus' conception.

163.  (True / False)  Mary needed to be saved by Jesus Christ — the difference is only in the mode and timing of her salvation.

✓ Answer: TRUE — there is only one Savior. Mary is the most perfectly redeemed creature, saved preventively (not remedially) by Christ's merits.

164.  (True / False)  Luke 1:34 ("How can this be, since I do not know man?") implies Mary had already committed herself to perpetual virginity.

✓ Answer: TRUE — if she expected normal conjugal life with Joseph, the question makes no sense. It only makes sense if she had already vowed virginity.

165.  (True / False)  "Praying to Mary" is the same act as "worshiping Mary."

✓ Answer: FALSE — for Catholics, praying to Mary means asking for her intercession, exactly as Paul asked others to pray for him (Romans 15:30). Worship (latria) is owed to God alone.

166.  (True / False)  Martin Luther rejected the Perpetual Virginity of Mary and the practice of asking for her intercession.

✓ Answer: FALSE — Luther encouraged asking for Mary's intercession and believed in her Perpetual Virginity. It was Calvin who removed Mary almost entirely from Protestant piety.

167.  (True / False)  Mary's dignity as Mother of God places her above the angels and all other saints in the hierarchy of heaven — including the Seraphim.

✓ Answer: TRUE — St. Thomas: "Mary's dignity is infinite, since she is the mother of an infinite divine Person." Pius XII: "Mary most holy is far above all creatures in dignity."
