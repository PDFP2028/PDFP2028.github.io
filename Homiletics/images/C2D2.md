# Proclaiming the Gospel

1. **Preparation** (also applies to proclaiming first or second readings)

   a. Read the Gospel.&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;┐
   
   b. Reflect on the Gospel.&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;│
   
   c. Practice reading aloud the Gospel.&emsp;├── **Do This At Home**
   
   d. Look up word pronunciations.&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;│
      
      (lector workbook, OSV guide)&emsp;&emsp;&emsp;&emsp; ┘
	  
   e. **Prior to Mass:** find the reading in the Book of Gospels and read it (proper Cycle, sense lines, multiple pages).
2. Celebrant’s Blessing: Say (sotto voce), **“Your blessing, Father.”** Make profound bow for the blessing; Make the sign of the Cross; Say **“Amen.”** (will address in liturgical practicums)
3. Procession to Ambo (will address in liturgical practicums – move intentionally/reverently)
4. **Introduction (said or sung)**

   a. The Greeting (wait for candle bearers to be set)
      - After placing the Book of Gospels on the Ambo, open it.
      - Put hands together, look at the people, then say: **“The Lord be with you.”**
      - Pause ~3 seconds before introducing the Gospel.

   b. Introducing the Gospel
      - Making the Sign of the Cross on the Book of Gospels with your thumb, say:<br>
        **"A reading from the Holy Gospel according to [Matthew, Mark, Luke, John]."**
      - Pronunciation: **Ă** reading …, not **Ā** reading …
      - Sign your forehead, lips, and breast with thumb during the response.

   c. Incense (optional – will address in liturgical practicums)
5. **Proclaiming the Gospel** (said or occasionally sung)

   a. **Posture & Eye Contact** (hands together vs. placing hands on the Book/Ambo)
   
   b. **Tempo & Pauses** (not too slow/not too fast, varying the tempo;
      pause at changes in scenes or theme, or embedded Scripture)
	  
   c. **Inflection & Emphasis** (vary tone, pitch, loudness to change meaning/show emotion;
      avoid extremes – not monotone nor dramatic reading)
	  
   d. **Handling mistakes** (minimize the situation – simply correct – no “oops” or “sorry”)
   
   e. **Amplification** (setting and using a microphone – learn to use your system)
6. **Closing Acclamation** (said or sung)

   a. Put hands together and look at the people.
   
   b. Pause ~3 seconds after finishing the Gospel before the saying the acclamation, **“The Gospel of the Lord.”**
   
   c. Wait until after all say, **“Praise to you, Lord Jesus Christ,”** before reverencing the Book of Gospels (when Bishop is celebrating, he may reverence the Book).
7. **Reverencing the Gospel**

   a. Raise the Book of Gospels slightly.
   
   b. Kiss the first page of the Gospel reading.
   
   c. Say (sotto voce),
      **"Through the words of the Gospel may our sins be wiped away."**
