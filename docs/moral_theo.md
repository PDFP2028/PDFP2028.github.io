## Week 1: August 22, 2026

**Topic 1:** Foundations of Moral Theology in the Old Testament (the Prophets, Ten Commandments) and Greek Philosophy
A. Book of Amos and Social Justice
B. Amos: Religion & Morality Are The Same
C. Ten Commandments: Blueprint for Morality and Religion
D. Aristotle and Morality

**Topic 2:** Foundations of Moral Theology in the New Testament (the Gospels, Beatitudes, Epistles of St. Paul) and the Apostolic Era (Didache, etc.)
A. Sermon on the Mount: Jesus Begins His Ministry with Emphasis on Morality
B. Gospel of Luke: Good Samaritan: To be a Disciple is to Love Neighbor
C. St. Paul's Letters to the Romans & Corinthians: Against Immoral Living in Greco-Roman Society
D. Didache: One of the Earliest Non-Biblical Apostolic Sources of Morality

**Readings:**<br>
Mattison ch. 1<br>
